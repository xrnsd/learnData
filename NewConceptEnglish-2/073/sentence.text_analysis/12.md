*surely* **→** certainly */* undoubtedly */* without doubt
*set up a record* **→** set a record */* establish a record */* break all previous records（语气略强）/ achieve a record（稍正式）
*for* **→** among （最自然，表示“在……之中”） */*  with */* as（so 稍偏书面）far as … are concerned (就……而言 */* 在……看来)
*dream of evading school* **→**  dream of avoiding school */* long to escape school */* are eager to dodge school（语气稍口语）
*dream of evading school* ❌ run away from school（容易理解成离家出走 */* hate school（情绪不同）
**→** He has surely set up a record, *as far as* the thousands of boys who dream of evading school *are concerned*.
**→** He has surely set up a record*, which* the thousands of boys who dream of evading school would agree on. *判断型从句*
* He **has surely* **set up* a record for the **thousands of* boys who dream of **evading school*. \U0001F4CC

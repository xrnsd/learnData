**Working rapidly in the darkness** 这里并不是“工作”，而是： **忙着、动作迅速地操作**
**change into + clothes** 换上、改穿（强调“换成另一套”）
同义改写：
1. Moving rapidly in the darkness, he soon changed into the dead man’s clothes.
2. Working fast in the dark, he quickly put on the dead man’s clothes.

**大本钟得名于本杰明·霍尔爵士，当新议会大厦正在建造时，他负责这座钟的建造工作。**
*takes its name from*  “得名于 / 名字来源于”

* Big Ben **takes its name from* Sir Benjamin Hall who was **responsible for* the **making of* the clock when the new Houses of Parliament were being built. \U0001F4CC

**which lasted** 非限制性定语从句，补充说明这次手术
**prove to be** “结果证明是... / 最终发现是... / 事实表明是...”
**because of the hard resin**  原因状语,“因为坚硬的树脂”
because of + 名词 / because + 句子
**which covered the skin** 定语从句，修饰 *resin*


* The ++operation+, which ++lasted+ for over four hours, **proved to be* very difficult **because of* the hard resin ++which+ ++covered+ the ++skin+. \U0001F4CC

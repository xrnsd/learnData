* **语法说明**
 **With great difficulty**方式状语,不是“和……一起”，而是“以……方式/带着……状态”
 *with care*（小心地）  *with anger*（愤怒地）  *with excitement*（兴奋地）

* **With great difficulty*, he ++climbed+ into the shop window to get the dress. \U0001F4CC
* With great difficulty, he climbe++d in+to the shop window to ge++t t+he dress. \U0001F444

**the way** 本身就可以带一个从句，表示“……的方式 / ……的样子”,所以从句中的关系代词 **that / in which** 常常省略。

* The ++assistant+ who ++served+ her did not like the ++way+ she was dressed. \U0001F4CC
* The assistant who serve++d+ her did no++t+ like the way she was dress++ed+. \U0001F444
* The assistant who served her did not like the way she **was dressed*. \U0001F92F

* *enjoy oneself* 是固定短语，表示“自得其乐、玩得高兴”。
* *making* 表示“同时做某事”或“结果是……”。 她玩得很开心，**并且（同时）让店员把东西都拿出来**。
* *before* 表示“在……之前”。 **making … before finally buying** 在最终买下那件衣服之前（她一直让店员拿东西）。
* *had first (asked for)* 表示“比过去的另一个动作更早发生”。 **had first asked for** = （她）最开始就问过的。
* She **enjoyed herself* ++making+ the assistant bring almost everything in the window ++before+ finally buying the dress she **had first* asked for. \U0001F4CC
* She enjoyed herself making the assistant **bring* almost everything in the window before **finally* buying the dress she had first ask++ed+ for. \U0001F444

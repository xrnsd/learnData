**astonished** 的意思是 **“震惊的、惊讶的、十分吃惊的”** 比 surprised 更强烈一些，带有“被惊到、愣住”的感觉。下面的句子可以替换理解：
* He was very surprised at what he found.（较弱）
* He was amazed at what he found.（差不多）

* He was ++astonish+ed ++at+ what he found. \U0001F9BB
* He was ++astonish+ed ++at+ what he found. \U0001F4CC
* He wa++s astonished+ a++t+ what he found. \U0001F444

**for** 因为 ， **have been confined** 表示持续的被困

* He had had a long and ++uncomfortable+ trip, for he had been ++confined+ to the wooden box for over eighteen hours. \U0001F9BB
* He ++had had+ a long and uncomfortable trip, for he ha++d been+ confined to the++ wooden box+ for over eighteen hours. \U0001F9BB
* He had had a long and ++uncomfortable+ trip, for he had been ++confined+ to the wooden box for over eighteen hours. \U0001F4CC

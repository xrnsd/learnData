**for + 时间段** 一般用于可数的、量化的时段，如 *for the whole morning* , *for two hours*, *for a long time*, *for three days*。
**all morning** 本身已经是一个完整自然的时间段表达，通常不与 **for** 连用,母语者几乎不会说 *for all morning*，听起来不地道。

* We have just move++d in+to a new house and I have been working hard all morning. \U0001F9BB
* We have just moved --into a- new house and I have been **working hard* **x for x* all morning. \U0001F92F
* We have just move++d in+to a new house and I have been working hard all morning. \U0001F444

**was being tested** 过去进行时 + 被动语态 : 当时正在被测试 / 正在接受考试
主动形式：*They were testing me for a driving licence.*

*I was tested for a driving licence for the third time.* 强调“结果 / 事实”（我考过一次）
*I was being tested for a driving licence for the third time.* 强调“过程 / 当时正在考试”

* I **was being tested* for a **driving licence* for the third time. \U0001F4CC

## fairly 怎么理解
**fairly soon** 相当快地 / 不久之后 / 很快,它不是“公正地”的意思，而是副词表示程度，比 soon 强一点，但比 very soon 弱一些。
* **In spite of this*, many people are ++confident+ that ‘The Revealer’ may ++reveal+ something of value ++fairly+ soon. \U0001F4CC
* --In spite of this-, many people are --confident- that ‘The Revealer’ may --reveal- something of --value- fairly soon. \U0001F648
* In spite ++of+ this, many people are confid++ent that+ ‘The Revealer’ may reveal something of value ++fairly+ soon. \U0001F444

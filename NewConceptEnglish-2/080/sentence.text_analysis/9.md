arrive **in** a country / city
arrive **at** a small place（station, airport, village 等）
*be taken to* **被带去某地**（take → 从出发地看）
*可替换为：* brought to（bring → 从目的地看）| escorted to（正式、陪同） | conveyed / transported to（书面、强调运输）
等价改写*请注意词性和词组*：
1. **On their arrival in England**, they were taken to the Crystal Palace by train.
2. **After arriving in England**, they were taken to the Crystal Palace.
3. **When they arrived in England**, they were taken to the Crystal Palace.
4. **They arrived** in England **and** were **taken to** the Crystal Palace by train.

* **On arriving in* England, they were **taken to* the Crystal Palace **by* train. \U0001F4CC

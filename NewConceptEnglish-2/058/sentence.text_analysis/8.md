**as** : 连词 **“因为”**，相当于 **because**。
**have been coming**  从过去一直到现在,动作反复发生或持续进行（游客不断来）

* He has **pointed out* that the tree is a **useful source of income* ++as+ ++tourists+ have **been coming* from **all parts of the country* to see it. \U0001F4CC
* He has **pointed* out tha++t t+he tree is a useful **source* of income as **tourists* have been coming from all parts of the country to see it. \U0001F444

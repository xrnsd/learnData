**wound(wind 过去式) one’s way** 是一个固定搭配，意思是： **以弯弯曲曲、迂回的方式前进** 通常强调路线不直、自然延伸）
> *A narrow path wound its way through the forest.* 一条狭窄的小路在森林中蜿蜒延伸。
> *The long queue wound its way around the building.* 长长的队伍绕着大楼蜿蜒排开。

* The stream wound its way across the field and ++then+ flowed right under their tent! \U0001F9BB
* The ++stream+ ++wound+ its way ++across+ the ++field+ and then ++flowed right+ under their ++tent+! \U0001F4CC
* ++The+ stream wound **its way across* ++the+ field and then **flowed right* under their tent! \U0001F92F
* The **stream wound* its way across the **field* and then **flowed right under* their tent! \U0001F444

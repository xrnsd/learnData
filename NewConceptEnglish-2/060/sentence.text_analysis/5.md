用一般现在时表达“将来计划/安排” ， **intend** 这个动词本身表达的是“计划、打算”，所以含义指向未来，下面是类似的例子：
I **intend** to go. → 我打算去（未来）
She **plans** to leave early. → 她打算早点离开（未来）
He **hopes** to visit you. → 他希望来看你（未来）

* She will be arriving this evening and \033[1;36mintends\033[0m to stay for a few days. \U0001F4CC
* She will be arriving this evening and **in*tends to stay for a few days. \U0001F92F

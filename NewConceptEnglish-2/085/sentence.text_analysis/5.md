**shall** → 正式, 带有“我们一定会……”“必将……”的意味, 常用于演讲,致辞,回忆性文字 > 所以这里可以理解为：我们一定会永远记住……
句子中有两个并列的 *for* 结构：**for his patience and understanding** / **for the kindly encouragement he gave us …**
**he gave us**（定语从句），修饰 **encouragement** ， 省略了关系代词, 完整形式是：the kindly encouragement **that he gave us**
++类似用法1+: *We shall never forget his kindness.* 我们永远不会忘记他的善良。
++类似用法2+: *She shall always be remembered for her courage.* 她将因勇气而被永远铭记。
++类似用法3+: *the help he gave me when I was in trouble*
++仿写1+: We shall always remember our teacher for his strictness and patience and for the helpful advice he gave us when we were confused.
我们将永远记得我们的老师，记住他的严格与耐心，以及在我们困惑时他给予我们的有益建议。
++仿写2+: We shall remember our parents for their care and understanding and for the constant support they gave us when we were in difficulty.
我们将记住我们的父母，记住他们的关怀与体谅，以及在我们遇到困难时给予我们的持续支持。

* We ++shall+ **all remember* Mr Page for his ++patience+ and ++understanding+ and for the **kindly encouragement* he gave us when we went so ++unwillingly+ to school. \U0001F4CC

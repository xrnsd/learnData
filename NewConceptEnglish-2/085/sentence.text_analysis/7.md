**a total of** 共
表达 *到将来某个时间为止 + 已经持续多久*，就必须用 *will*,但下面搭配意思有区别：
**will have taught for forty years** 强调 *结果*
**will have been teaching for forty years** 强调 *持续过程 + 时间长度*
++类似写法1+： *By next year, he will have been working here for ten years.* 到明年为止，他就在这里工作十年了。
++类似写法1+： *On her birthday, she will have been living in London for five years.* 到她生日那天，她在伦敦已经住了五年。
++仿写1+： It is a strange coincidence that the day before her wedding, she will have been working for the company for twenty years.
很巧的是，在她结婚的前一天，她在这家公司工作正好满二十年。
++仿写2+： It is an interesting coincidence that on his last day at school, he will have been studying English for ten years.
很有意思的是，在他离开学校的最后一天，他学习英语正好满十年。

* It is a ++curious+ ++coincidence+ that **the day before* his ++retirement+, Mr Page **will have been teaching* for **a total of* forty years. \U0001F4CC

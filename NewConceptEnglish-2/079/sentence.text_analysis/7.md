这里的 **and** 不是连句子，而是**并列两个不定式动作**。
① *to keep calm* 保持冷静 , ② *to get off the plane quietly* 安静地下机
① **第一个动作的 to 一定要有** , ② 后面并列的动作可以保留 to（书面）, 也可以省略 to（口语）
**as soon as** = 一……就……

* While we were waiting to ++land+, an **air hostess* told us to **keep calm* and to **get off* the plane ++quietly+ as soon as it had **touched down*. \U0001F4CC

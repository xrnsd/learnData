* **had enough time to do sth**：有足够的时间去做某事, 加上**just**  >>>  **“刚刚好有足够的时间去做某事（几乎来不及）”**
++example:+ *We had enough time to catch the train.* 我们有足够的时间赶上火车。
++example:+ *We had **just** enough time to escape.* 我们**刚刚好**逃了出来。
++example:+ *She had just enough time to sit down when the meeting began.*  她刚坐下，会议就开始了。
++example:+ *We had just enough time to get inside when it started raining.*  我们刚进屋就下雨了。
**complete a circle** 转了一整圈 / 绕了一圈  >>>  **again** *completed a circle*  又转了一整圈
++example:+ *The plane again took off into the dark sky.*  飞机再次起飞，消失在夜空中。
++example:+ *The machine again failed after a few minutes.*  机器几分钟后又一次出故障。
**when** 在这里不是“当……的时候”那么简单，而是：**“就在……那一刻” / “刚一……就…… / 刚刚来得及做完 A，B 就发生了 ”**
++The Chinese understanding is that+:就在船再次转完一圈的时候，他们刚刚好有足够的时间游出危险。

* They **had just had enough time to* swim **out of danger* when the boat **again completed* a ++circle+. \U0001F4CC

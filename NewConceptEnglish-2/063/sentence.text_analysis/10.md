++Below are three synonymous rewrites+
> **On their way home, he asked Jenny whether she had enjoyed the speech.**
> **On the way home, he asked Jenny, “Did you enjoy the speech?”**
> **On the way home, he asked Jenny if she had liked the speech.**
enjoyed是*过去的过去*的动作，唯一标准手段就是过去完成时，所以这里必须用had

* **On the way home*, he asked Jenny if she had enjoyed the speech. \U0001F4CC
* **On the way home*, he asked Jenny if she **had enjoyed* the speech. \U0001F92F

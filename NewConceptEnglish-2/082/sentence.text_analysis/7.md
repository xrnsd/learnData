++还原为从句+： Because the fisherman realized that this was no ordinary fish , he …
**not an ordinary fish** / **no ordinary fish**（更强调，强烈否定，相当于 **not an ordinary fish at all**）
**make every effort to do sth**  想尽一切办法去做 / **make every effort ++not+ to do sth** 想尽一切办法去避免 / **in any way** 任何方式
英语（尤其书面语）常把： Because / When / As + 主语 + 动词  **压缩成** 现在分词短语
++可压缩条件+： *1.*分词短语的逻辑主语 = 主句主语 *2.*动作在逻辑上先发生或同时发生
++模板1+：**Realizing / Knowing / Seeing + that 从句** *Realizing that time was limited, he spoke more quickly.* 意识到时间有限，他说话加快了。
++模板2+：**现在分词 + 主句（表示原因）* *Seeing that no one was watching, he left quietly. / Knowing the risks, they decided to continue.*
++模板3+：**否定强调型** She made every effort not to disturb him in any way. / He tried not to reveal the truth in any form.

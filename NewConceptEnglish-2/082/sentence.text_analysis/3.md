**stories told by seamen** 完整还原为从句是：stories **which were told by seamen**
英语在**书面语**中常把 which / that + be + 过去分词 *>>>  压缩成  >>>* 过去分词短语, 含义完全一样，但更简洁、正式。
stories **that were told by seamen** *>>>  压缩成  >>>* stories **told by seamen**
* ++同类结构+: books **written by children**  /  pictures **taken during the war**  /  letters **sent from abroad**
* ++核心信息+: many of these ‘monsters’ are simply strange fish  /  **have been sighted** 被看到  /  **at times** = 有时、偶尔、曾经
* ++模板1+(让步+主句): Though people have often + 评价 + 名词短语（分词定语）, it is now known that + 核心事实（含定语从句）
  Though many people doubted reports **published in the past**, it is now known that some of these events **which were once dismissed** actually occurred.
  尽管许多人曾经怀疑过去发表的那些报道，但现在已经知道，其中一些一度被否定的事件实际上确实发生过。
* ++模板2+(分词+定语从句并用)： Many of the + 名词 + 过去分词定语 + which + 从句 + are / were + 事实
  Many of the paintings **found in the cave**, which **had long been ignored**, are now considered valuable.
  许多在洞穴中发现的绘画作品，过去长期被忽视，现在却被认为很有价值。
